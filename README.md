# Fitly-Body-Scanning-Feature-for-Online-Clothing-Platform
The problem:
Online shoppers can’t try clothes on, and size labels (S/M/L) mean different things at different sellers. Many shoppers end up guessing their size or receive clothes that don’t fit, even when they order their usual size.

How Fitly works:
Browse and filter: the customer picks a clothing category and filters by colour, price, material and more.
Get suggestions: Zalora shows matching products from different brands.
Use Fitly: the customer scans their body with the phone camera and chooses a fit preference: Fitted, Regular or Baggy.
Get a recommendation: Fitly compares the body measurements and fit preference with each product’s garment measurements (in cm), then recommends a size across brands whose garments have matching measurements.

Key features:
Camera-based body scan with no extra hardware
Three fit preferences: Fitted, Regular, Baggy
Cross-brand size recommendation based on real garment measurements, not S/M/L labels
Privacy-first design: only estimated measurements are stored, scan images are not kept, and users can delete their data

Users:
Customers: online fashion shoppers who want to choose the right size with confidence
Sellers and brand partners: provide garment measurements so Fitly can match sizes

Status and disclaimer:
This is a design-only student project. It does not work fully yet since it is only a figma wireframe.
